August review: 12 · plus 65 requests for directions

The August changes worked in the direction we wanted: your ad now wins a bigger share of the searches near you, and fewer of them are lost to competing ads. But the share lost because your daily budget ran out has grown, and about a third of the budget still goes to people typing "Mofongos" by name. Those diners were already on their way to you. The people we most want to reach, those searching "caribbean food near me" or "cuban food near me" who have never heard of mofongo, were only being reached by accident.

What we changed on November 5

Three ads, each written for a different kind of diner

Until now one ad had to answer around 40 different searches, from "mofongos" to "caribbean lunch specials". Google rated most of the keywords it scored 6 out of 10 for relevance, which is a large part of why your ad reached the top of the page only about 1 time in 10. Each kind of search now gets an ad that speaks to it directly, and every dish named in these ads is on your online menu today.

Done, and what needs your OK

Three changes made, one decision for you

The first three items cost nothing extra. They change where your current budget goes. The new ads go live as soon as Google finishes its standard review, which usually takes about a business day.

✓

Split one ad into three, each matched to its searcher

Discovery, Explorers and your name each have their own ad and keywords. A closer match between the search and the ad is how Google decides who ranks higher, so this goes straight at the 51% lost to ad rank.

A

Raise the daily budget from $3.30 to $5.00

30% of the searches you could have won last month were missed only because the day's budget had run out, up from 27% in August, and on 12 days it was half or more. The two new ad groups also share this same budget. At $3.30 they can only reach new guests by taking budget from the searches that already work. At today's average of 33¢ a visit, the extra $1.70 a day pays for roughly five more visits a day. $5.00 is the standard starting budget we recommend for a single-location restaurant.
